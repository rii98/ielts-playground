  Task Achievement:
    Band 9 — Fully satisfies all requirements; all three bullet points fully and relevantly extended; purpose unmistakable; tone consistently appropriate throughout.
    Band 8 — Covers all requirements sufficiently; all bullet points clearly presented, highlighted and extended; purpose clear; tone consistent and appropriate, with only rare lapses.
    Band 7 — Covers all requirements; all bullet points presented and highlighted but could be more fully extended; purpose clear; tone consistent and appropriate to the reader.
    Band 6 — Addresses the requirements; all bullet points presented but one may be inadequately covered or irrelevant; purpose generally clear; there may be inconsistencies in tone.
    Band 5 — Generally addresses the task; format may be inappropriate in places; bullet points presented but inadequately covered, or one missing; purpose may be unclear; tone variable and sometimes inappropriate.
    Band 4 — Attempts the task but does not cover all bullet points; format may be inappropriate; purpose unclear; tone may be inappropriate for the reader.
  Coherence and Cohesion:
    Band 9 — Cohesion is used so skilfully it attracts no attention; paragraphing is handled effortlessly.
    Band 8 — Sequences information and ideas logically; manages all aspects of cohesion well; uses paragraphing sufficiently and appropriately.
    Band 7 — Organises ideas logically with clear progression; uses a range of cohesive devices appropriately, if with some under- or over-use; each paragraph has a clear central topic.
    Band 6 — Arranges information coherently with clear overall progression; uses cohesive devices effectively but cohesion within/between sentences can be faulty or mechanical; referencing not always clear; paragraphing may be illogical.
    Band 5 — Some organisation but no clear overall progression; cohesive devices inadequate, inaccurate or over-used; repetitive for lack of referencing/substitution; paragraphing absent or inadequate.
    Band 4 — Information and ideas not arranged coherently; no clear progression; some basic cohesive devices, often inaccurate or repetitive; may not write in paragraphs, or their use is confusing.
  Lexical Resource:
    Band 9 — Wide range used naturally, flexibly and precisely; only rare minor slips of the kind fluent speakers make.
    Band 8 — Wide resource used fluently and flexibly to convey precise meaning; skilful use of uncommon items with only occasional inaccuracy; rare minor spelling/word-formation errors.
    Band 7 — Sufficient range for flexibility and precision; uses some less common vocabulary with awareness of style and collocation; occasional errors in word choice, spelling or word formation.
    Band 6 — Adequate range for the task; attempts less common vocabulary with some inaccuracy; some spelling/word-formation errors, but they do not impede communication.
    Band 5 — Limited range, only minimally adequate for the task; noticeable spelling/word-formation errors that may cause the reader some difficulty.
    Band 4 — Only basic vocabulary, used repetitively or inappropriately for the task; limited control of word formation and spelling; errors may cause strain for the reader.
  Grammatical Range and Accuracy:
    Band 9 — Wide range of structures used with full flexibility and accuracy; only rare minor slips.
    Band 8 — Wide range of structures; the majority of sentences are error-free; only very occasional errors or inappropriacies.
    Band 7 — Uses a variety of complex structures; produces frequent error-free sentences; good control of grammar and punctuation with a few errors.
    Band 6 — Mix of simple and complex forms; some errors in grammar and punctuation, but they rarely reduce communication.
    Band 5 — Limited range of structures; attempts complex sentences but these are less accurate than simple ones; frequent errors, and punctuation may be faulty; errors can cause the reader difficulty.
    Band 4 — Very limited range of structures with only rare subordinate clauses; some structures are accurate but errors predominate; punctuation is often faulty.
